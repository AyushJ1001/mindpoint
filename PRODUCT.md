# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mind Point serves adults in India. Students purchase or receive Course Enrollments, complete structured learning and assessments, ask Faculty questions, submit private feedback, and receive proof of completion. Faculty teach assigned Courses and review Student work. Administrators control publication, access, records, and compliance.

## Product purpose

Mind Point combines its course storefront with an authenticated learning workspace. Success means a Student can understand what to do next, complete every published requirement, get Faculty support, and receive a verifiable Certificate without staff assembling records by hand.

## Positioning

Each Enrollment follows a versioned Curriculum with explicit Completion evidence. Course progress, Faculty review, exceptions, and Certificate issuance remain traceable rather than depending on informal messages and file delivery.

## Operating context

Students use the responsive web app on phones and desktop browsers. Courses may be self-paced or batch-based. Activities include Reading, Media, External resource, Quiz, Assignment, and Feedback form. Course discussion, private questions, Notifications, grading, accommodations, and Certificates share the same workspace.

## Capabilities and constraints

- The LMS lives inside the existing Next.js and Convex application.
- Enrollments may come from purchases or Administrator entry.
- A Course has versioned Modules and typed Learning activities.
- Access, release, progress, completion, and Certificate state are distinct.
- Supporting resources require rights, access, expiry, attribution, and accessibility review.
- The MVP is English and responsive web only. Native apps, multiple school tenants, built-in conferencing, minors, accredited qualifications, AI tutoring, plagiarism detection, and proctoring are out of scope.
- Current product claims, Terms, file delivery, and course records need migration review before LMS launch.

## Brand commitments

The product name is The Mind Point. Preserve the incumbent calm lavender palette, warm neutral surfaces, Fraunces display type, Plus Jakarta Sans interface type, logo, and supportive plain-language voice. Do not invent clinical, accreditation, employment, or global-recognition claims.

## Evidence on hand

The repository contains the current public site, course catalogue, account and Administrator pages, course data, Terms, FAQs, testimonials, certificate sample, and learning-related PDFs. These assets show existing operations but do not prove accessibility, rights clearance, accreditation, or LMS completion behavior.

## Product principles

- Show the Student the next valid action and why anything is blocked.
- Never fabricate, hide, or silently discard learning evidence.
- Keep private support, accommodations, feedback, and identity details within their approved audience.
- Make accessibility and lawful resource access part of publication.
- Automate completion and Certificate issuance only from published rules and retained evidence.

## Accessibility & inclusion

The responsive LMS targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA and maps relevant requirements to IS 17802. Critical Student journeys must work with keyboard, screen readers, zoom, reflow, increased text spacing, high contrast, reduced motion, and common mobile inputs. Required content needs an equivalent accessible route.
