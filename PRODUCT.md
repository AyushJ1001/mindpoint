# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The Mind Point serves psychology students, aspiring mental health professionals, working practitioners, career changers, and people seeking structured personal support in India.

## Product Purpose

The product brings psychology education, practical training, supervised learning, therapy, career support, and learner resources into one connected experience. The public website should help a first-time visitor understand the right path and take a useful next step: enroll in a program, open their learner space, or contact TMP.

## Positioning

TMP combines structured psychology learning with practical application and human guidance in one accessible Indian learning ecosystem.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare course formats, dates, prices, offers, credentials, and support paths before enrolling. Enrolled users return through the account and learning experience to access their learning journey and progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The website sells live, self-paced, certificate, diploma, internship, supervision, worksheet, therapy, and career-support offerings.
- The TMP Learning Portal exists as a dedicated learner experience; its implementation continues to evolve, while public marketing presents it as an active part of the TMP ecosystem at the user's direction.
- Course, enrollment, cart, payment, authentication, and learner data behavior must remain intact during marketing and visual work.
- Public claims must use supplied or repository-backed evidence; the site must not invent learner counts, outcomes, reviews, or product guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is The Mind Point. Preserve the botanical TMP logo, Cormorant display typography, Manrope body typography, coastal imagery, and the deep-teal, sea-glass, warm-sand, and ivory palette. The brand promise is “Learn · Grow · Heal · Belong,” with a calm, credible, human voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand assets: `public/tmp-botanical-mark.webp` and `public/tmp-coastal-hero.webp`.
- Accreditation assets and existing product copy identify MSME registration, ISO 9001:2015, and IAOTH accreditation.
- The repository contains existing program, enrollment, offer, payment, learner account, and support flows.
- Existing testimonials require content-owner verification before they are strengthened or used as quantified proof.

## Product Principles

- Help every visitor identify a relevant path quickly.
- Turn interest into a clear enrollment or conversation action.
- Explain professional value with concrete, verifiable detail.
- Present learning as structured and credible without making it feel cold.
- Keep the full experience recognisably TMP across marketing and learner surfaces.
