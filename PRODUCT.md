# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Adult Students in India who have purchased or received a manual enrollment and need one clear place to learn, submit work, ask for help, track completion, and receive a Certificate.
- Assigned Faculty who answer Questions, review assessments, approve Completion where configured, and maintain learning resources within their Course or batch scope.
- Administrators who author and publish Curricula, activate Enrollments, manage access and staffing, and resolve operational exceptions.

## Product Purpose

The Mind Point adds a responsive learning-management system to the existing application. Success means a Student can move through a published Curriculum from first activity to verified Completion without staff reconstructing learning evidence by hand.

## Positioning

The LMS connects each Enrollment to an immutable Published Curriculum version and preserves the evidence behind access, progress, assessment, Completion, and Certificate decisions as the Course evolves.

## Operating Context

Courses are organized as ordered Modules containing typed Learning activities. Administrators publish a reviewed Curriculum separately from activating Student Enrollments. Students work from a Course workspace; Faculty work from a permission-scoped review queue; Administrators work from a publication-readiness desk. Existing purchased and manually created Enrollments must migrate without losing commercial or learning history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- MVP activity types are Reading, Media, External resource, Quiz, Assignment, and Feedback form.
- MVP includes release and prerequisite rules, progress evidence, grading and resubmission, public and private Questions, Feedback, Notifications, accessibility and Rights controls, Completion, Faculty approval where configured, and verifiable Certificates.
- The first release is an English responsive web application for adults. Native apps, minors, multi-school tenancy, accredited qualifications, AI tutoring, plagiarism detection, proctoring, and built-in conferencing are outside scope.
- Typed Live sessions and verified Attendance belong to a later release.
- Formal counsel validation is outside the product-planning effort. Mind Point owns versioned internal policies and must not present them as legal approval.

## Brand Commitments

Always use the full name “The Mind Point.” The visual identity is deep botanical teal, soft sage, warm ivory, and sand, led by the supplied TMP leaf mark and full wordmark. Interfaces should feel like a calm waterside sanctuary: spacious, grounded, natural, and quietly flowing without becoming decorative or vague. Product language is supportive, direct, and specific; it must not invent accreditation, career-outcome, or legal-compliance claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- The implementation sequence and acceptance boundaries are in `docs/lms-release-plan.md`.
- Approved interaction evidence exists in the Student workspace, Faculty workspace, Curriculum authoring, and Role dashboard prototype branches linked from the Wayfinder map.
- Demonstration Course, Student, assessment, and progress content is synthetic until production records are connected and must be labeled accordingly.

## Product Principles

- Put the next valid action in front of each Role without duplicating the authoritative workflow.
- Separate access from progress and preserve prior evidence through every correction.
- Publish immutable Curriculum versions; make material changes through a new version.
- Fail closed on missing permission, ownership, Rights, accessibility, or policy decisions.
- Introduce existing Students through dry runs, a small pilot, and bounded rollout waves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the product acceptance target across Student, Faculty, Administrator, public Certificate verification, and messaging flows. Required content needs an accessible form or equivalent route, and Accommodation details remain private and permission-scoped.
