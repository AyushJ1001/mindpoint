Questions

A few honest answers.

Is this a recognised degree?

No. Our certificates document completion of a training programme — they are not a degree, licence or accreditation. We say so plainly, and we verify every certificate publicly.

Do I need a psychology background?

Not always. Some programs assume no prior training; others are built for students and practitioners. Each program page states who it's for.

How do the live classes work?

Cohorts are small and run live every week. If you miss one, the recording is available to your cohort for the access window.

What if I'm not sure where to start?

Start with one calm conversation. It's a single session with a licensed professional — a low-pressure way to get oriented.
