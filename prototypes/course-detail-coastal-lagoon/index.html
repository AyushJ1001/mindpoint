19 Jan – 11 Mar 2027 · ₹3,499 · 30 seats

Most courses explain the model. This one makes you work it.

You will read real cases, argue about them, get them wrong, and try again — in a small cohort where that is expected rather than embarrassing.

What you will be able to do

  1. iExplain the relationship between thoughts, emotions and behaviours, and where CBT, REBT and CBMT differ.
  2. iiSpot cognitive distortions and irrational beliefs, and challenge them without arguing with the person.
  3. iiiUse behavioural activation and exposure strategies, and know when not to.
  4. ivRegulate your own reactions before you try to help someone regulate theirs.
  5. vStructure a session: rapport, agenda, and measurable goals.
  6. viApply it to real situations, and say plainly where your competence ends.

Who it is for

  • Psychology students and graduates who want the practical half, not just the theory
  • Counsellors who want a structured method they can actually run
  • People who have tried self-study and need to be taught by someone

Who it is not for

  • Anyone looking for a licence, a degree, or supervised clinical practice
  • Anyone who wants to watch lectures and never speak
The difference was being made to try it, badly, in front of people who were also trying it badly.
