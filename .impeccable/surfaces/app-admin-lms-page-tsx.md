# Administrator LMS release desk

## Scope and mode

Operate-mode Administrator surface at `app/admin/lms/page.tsx`, extending the existing Admin shell with a focused Curriculum release and Enrollment activation desk.

## Direction contract

- THESIS: Publication is a deliberate handoff from editable Draft to immutable Published Curriculum, followed by separate Enrollment activation; refuse a dashboard of detached metrics.
- OWN-WORLD: Carry The Mind Point botanical teal, warm paper, sage readiness fields, waterline dividers, and direct operational typography into the existing Admin shell.
- STORY: Choose a Course, create or inspect its Draft, add ordered Modules and typed activities, clear visible readiness gates, publish, then explicitly activate eligible Enrollments and assign Faculty.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: The existing Admin navigation remains; Course and Curriculum selection sit above a three-region release basin with outline, active authoring form, and readiness/activation bank.
- FORM: Production graduation of the approved Administrator Release desk under seed `5b7b6338`; publication and activation stay visibly separate.
- FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Only server-verified Administrators may mutate Curricula, publication, activation, or Faculty scope. Published versions remain immutable. Rights approval and accessible alternatives block publication where required. Every privileged state change is audited where the backend model supports it. This milestone authors the four production-supported paths—Reading, Media, External resource, and Assignment. Quiz and Feedback stay unavailable until their dedicated attempt, grading, anonymity, and receipt evidence models ship; the release desk must not imply otherwise.
