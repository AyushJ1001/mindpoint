# Role dashboards

Mode: Operate

Purpose: Let a signed-in person choose an active Role and find the highest-priority permitted action across Courses or operations.

Chosen composition: Role desk. Use a stable top role switcher, role-specific navigation, one dominant next-action region, a short attention list, and a narrow watchlist. Preserve the incumbent lavender and warm-neutral identity. Avoid metric-card grids, generic feeds, and controls that imply permissions the active Role lacks.

Responsive behavior: Keep the role switcher visible. Collapse the navigation rail. Stack the watchlist after the attention list. Preserve labels for every status and do not rely on color.
