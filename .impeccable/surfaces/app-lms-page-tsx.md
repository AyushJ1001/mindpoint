# Student LMS workspace

## Scope and mode

Operate-mode authenticated Student workspace at `app/lms/page.tsx`, graduating the approved Student Study desk from the LMS preview into live Clerk and Convex data.

## Audience, job, and action

An enrolled Student needs to find the next available activity, understand why later work is locked, complete eligible reading or evidence tasks, and ask for private support without leaving the Course context.

## Direction contract

- THESIS: One enrolled Student follows the next valid learning current; refuse a generic dashboard grid.
- OWN-WORLD: Inherit the Tidal Learning Sanctuary—deep botanical teal, quiet sage, warm ivory, fine waterline rules, generous breathing room, and the supplied TMP leaf mark.
- STORY: Sign in, select an activated Enrollment, resume the next available activity, produce evidence or confirm completion, and ask for help while preserving Curriculum provenance.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact teal brand-and-status current spans the top; Course and progress sit below; the Course map forms the left bank, the active activity owns the center, and progress plus private help form the right bank.
- FORM: Production graduation of the approved Student Study desk selected under seed `5b7b6338`; activity changes settle quickly while the progress current remains the single authored motion.
- FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Content and constraints

Use real identity-derived Enrollments and published Curriculum data only. Never expose locked activity content. Completion and submission controls must represent real backend mutations, with honest loading, success, error, empty, unconfigured, and awaiting-activation states. Keep quiz and feedback actions visibly unavailable until their production handlers exist.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop uses a three-region basin. Below the structural breakpoint, Course selection and activity navigation precede the active work; progress and help follow inline. No horizontal scrolling is required for primary tasks.

## Memorable moment

Completing an activity visibly advances the calm progress current and releases the next prerequisite activity without disorienting the Student.
