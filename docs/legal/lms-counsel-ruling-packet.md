# Mind Point LMS counsel ruling packet

Prepared for qualified Indian counsel. This document requests product-specific legal decisions. It is not legal advice and does not supply the requested rulings.

## Purpose

Mind Point plans to add an adult-only, English, responsive web learning management system to its existing Indian course storefront. Students may purchase or receive Enrollments, use versioned learning content, submit assessments, ask questions, attend externally hosted live sessions, and receive course-completion Certificates.

The product team has settled its operating model and conservative controls. Counsel must now state which controls are required, which wording Mind Point must publish or obtain, what periods apply, and which planned uses remain prohibited or uncertain.

Keep privileged advice outside the public GitHub repository. The public implementation summary should contain the approved product rules without counsel's privileged analysis.

## Product assumptions to confirm

- Mind Point is a private Indian business serving adults in India.
- Courses are non-accredited unless a future, separately reviewed arrangement says otherwise.
- A Course has versioned Curricula containing Modules and typed Learning activities.
- Students may create Q&A posts. Faculty and Administrators moderate them.
- Courses may include copyrighted files, external links, provider embeds, and recordings.
- Live teaching uses approved external conferencing providers. The LMS stores schedules, attendance evidence, links, and any approved recording.
- Required learning evidence may include Quiz attempts, Assignment versions, grading, Completion records, Waivers, and Certificate records.
- The application uses third-party identity, database, payment, email, file, analytics, and conferencing providers. The final provider register will name their entities, regions, subprocessors, and contracts.
- Existing Students may have older promises about access, downloads, support, extensions, refunds, transfers, certificates, recognition, or professional outcomes.

Counsel should correct any assumption that is incomplete or legally material.

## Sign-off checklist

- Counsel has identified their name, firm, jurisdiction, engagement date, and scope in the private memo.
- Every ruling ID has a conclusion, authority, effective date, implementable control, and review trigger.
- Counsel has supplied exact wording or clear drafting instructions where the product needs copy.
- Every record category has a minimum, maximum or deletion trigger, and legal-hold rule.
- Counsel has listed facts they did not verify and conditions outside product control.
- Mind Point has approved a public implementation summary that does not waive privilege.

- The release owner has linked the approved summary to the final legal-rules and implementation-plan tickets.
